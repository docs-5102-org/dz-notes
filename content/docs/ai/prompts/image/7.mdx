# 工具类插画提示词

适合生成产品介绍页、功能区块和运营物料常用的工具插画。

## 模板

```text
clean flat illustration, open laptop on a desk,
document management interface on screen, checklist elements,
coffee cup and office accessories nearby, blue and white palette,
business productivity theme, modern SaaS illustration style
```

## 常见变体

- 项目管理：加入 `kanban board`, `team collaboration`
- 表单工具：加入 `form builder`, `approval workflow`
- 数据平台：加入 `charts`, `dashboard widgets`, `data cards`
